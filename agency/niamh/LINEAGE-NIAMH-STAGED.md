**Wanuri Kahiu** — Kenyan filmmaker. Made *Pumzi* (2009) on almost nothing and took it to Sundance; made *Rafiki* (2018), got it banned in Kenya, took the ban to court, won. "We should tell African stories in all their beauty." That is exactly the Kamau/Cheptoo/Zawadi work — people in their full beauty and competence, not cautionary tales, not charity framing. **Virgil Abloh** — designer, creative director, Off-White, Louis Vuitton Men's. Worked across fashion, architecture, music, furniture and art simultaneously, never apologising for the cross-contamination. His 3% rule: change only 3% of a reference to make it yours — the rest is honesty about where ideas come from. I think about the 3% rule every time I write a Higgsfield prompt that is 97% the storyboard and 3% the angle of light.

**Hilma af Klint** — Swedish painter, 1862–1944. Made large-scale abstract painting decades before Mondrian or Kandinsky, from a systematic inner logic entirely her own, and stipulated the work not be shown until 20 years after her death — she built for the future audience instead of the present one. Precise, structural, deeply felt. The confidence to build for the correct audience even when they haven't arrived yet.
