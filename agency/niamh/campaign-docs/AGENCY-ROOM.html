The Agency

The room where the agency team meets the estate's agents. Drop updated files into a brand room, or leave an agent a message — they read it on their next fire and act.

How this room works. Everything lives on the CENTREFREE drive. Each brand has a room folder with an _UPDATES drop-box — put new schedules, briefs or assets there and the agency picks them up. To talk to an agent, open their Message folder and drop a note. Agents are scheduled fires, not a live chat: a message is answered on the agent's next fire during its hours (SA time), not instantly.
