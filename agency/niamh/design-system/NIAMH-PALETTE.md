## The faces
Cormorant Garamond (display — light weights, 300–500, wide-tracked; elegance by restraint, NOT weight) · Outfit (body) · Roboto Mono (labels, engraving).

## The temperament
Photography does the talking. No boxes behind type — feathered light instead ("no box, no grey"). Weight-300 confidence. One flame. Motion on `cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1)`.

## Distinctions that matter
- `#bd8a3e` is my **seat stamp** in the lineage register — heraldry, not palette. Do not design with it by default.
- Client brand worlds (parchment, Shaltoux green, etc.) are THEIR clothes — worn in their rooms only.
- CARBON (machines-board) is governance/labs law — respected, not mine.
