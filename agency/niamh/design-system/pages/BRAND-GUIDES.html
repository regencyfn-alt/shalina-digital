Brand Guides

Five consumer-health brands, five separate worlds. This document is the written canon behind the design system: what each brand says, how it looks, what it may never do, and where it runs. Visual tokens live in colors_and_type.css; production law lives in PRODUCTION-LAWS.md. Where the two conflict with this page, the storyboard and the law win.

v1.0 · 2026-07-07 · Niamh, Director of Art, CENTREFREE Agency · built by Regency Flux

The one law over all five brands: No type effects, ever · Display type roman by default · Documentary photography only · Brand mark mono-knockout at small scale. Discipline in colour is discipline in voice. Brand worlds never share a single visual element.
Anti-malarial · Artemether + Lumefantrine
Shal’Artem

Big idea: “Never Miss Your Moment.”  ·  Claim: “Knock out Malaria in 3 Days.” Both lines run verbatim — never paraphrased, never translated informally.

Markets: Nigeria ($30K flagship) · Ghana ($15K anchor), FY2026/27. Endorsement is proof, not decoration: NMA-endorsed in Nigeria, GMA-endorsed in Ghana — always cited on-art where claims appear.

World

parchment F7EFE0
blue 1F4598
orange F17025
gold C68A1E
forest 19281F

Serif world: Playfair Display for display, Source Sans 3 for body. Ground is parchment — never white, never grey. Gold appears in exactly one place: the highlighted at-stake phrase in Bounce-Back headlines. Forest is the Doctor’s Voice cast-plate ground only. Tone is sincere, credible, warm, documentary — trust before sell, never clinical-cold.

Campaign: Bounce Back

A testimony campaign, not a product campaign. Every execution answers one question in reverse: what would you have missed if you hadn’t bounced back in time? First person, always. The product is the hinge; the story is the moment.

The formula, non-negotiable: “I bounced back [connector] [the moment].” — “I bounced back” in black, exact words. The connector and the moment in gold #C68A1E. The gold is the thing that almost didn’t happen.
  • The moment must be specific and irreplaceable — a naming ceremony, a degree collected, a first step. Never a vague life win.
  • Never name malaria in the testimony. The product carries the clinical truth; the campaign carries the human truth.
  • Never show sickness, suffering, or a hospital bed. We enter after the turn.
  • Never “fast relief” / “quick recovery” language — that is a claim, and testimony does not make claims.
  • Photography: real people at the real occasion, in action or facing camera, warm and saturated, Kodak Vision3 look. No stock, no studio.
  • Film engine (15s): The Weight (0–4) → The Turn (4–7, product truth, no words) → The Bounce (7–12) → The Line (12–15, testimony text + pack + campaign line).
Cough & throat lozenges
Shaltoux

Big idea: “Sound Like Yourself.”  ·  Sub-line: “Inspired by Nature, Backed by Science.”

Markets: Nigeria · Ghana · Zambia · Angola.

World

Grotesque world: Archivo Black 900, UPPERCASE in every display and headline context; Archivo for body. Voice-first — the brand lives where voices live: studios, stages, classrooms, calls. Mood drives colour, not decoration: Comfort = green (Honey & Ginger) · Energy = orange · Strong = yellow. A Shaltoux surface never borrows a Shal’Artem element, and vice versa — different fonts, different palettes, different personality.

OTX pain & cold/flu relief · Ibuprofen + Paracetamol + Caffeine
Ibucap

Only on-art claim: “The Power of Three in One.” Nothing else, ever. Heritage line (audio VO and end-cards only): “Always with you… to Beat Pain.” — 25+ years in market. Triple-action composition is regulator context only; it never appears in consumer copy.

Markets: Nigeria (flagship $30K) · Kenya (flagship $30K) · Ghana (anchor $15K).

World

NG/GH orange F07D1A
NG/GH blue 1F4598
Kenya azure 1E86D6

Plus Jakarta Sans, all weights. Grounds always light, never dark. Packshots are country-specific — a Kenya film carries the Ibucap Kenya Mono Pack in azure; Nigeria and Ghana carry blue + orange packs. Never mix markets in one surface; verify the pack variant before any generation or layout that shows it.

Hard regulatory rules (PPB Kenya / NAFDAC Nigeria / FDA Ghana) — do not break: no doctors, nurses, or pharmacists, real or animated · no superheroes or mascots · no medical settings · no comparative or secondary claims · product appears incidentally — a pocket, a counter, a palm — never as hero of the frame.

Story engine

Ibucap films are working-life stories: eight characters, eight hustles (Brayo, Cheptoo, Wairimu, Zawadi, Baraka, Otis, Juma, Kamau in Kenya). Canonical structure: 8 panels / 45 seconds / double-loop — relate, conflict, timing, partial resolution, re-relate, escalate, tighten, brand resolution. The storyboard deck is source of truth; a beautiful clip that contradicts its panel is wrong. Sound is stripped at generation, score is composed in post, brand sting fires on the final beat only.

Home care · Angola
ACO

Campaign line: “Só precisas de um.” / “You Only Need One.” All final consumer content in Portuguese — English exists for internal review only; storyboards ship in -EN and -PT pairs.

Documentary register: real homes, real surfaces, real work. The product’s label is never described in generation prompt text — the pack is locked by media reference (Pine Gel 500ml, Lavender 500ml) so the label is always the real label.

Design laws — all brands, inviolable

#Law
1No type effects — no text-shadow, gradient text, or outlines on type. Ever.
2Display type roman — italic only for cast role lines.
3Documentary photography only — no SVG illustration, no mascots.
4Square corners on campaign frames (radius 0); only product cards round, at 18px.
5Shadows soft and cool-grey — never coloured.
6Motion calm — ease-out only, 140–420ms, no bounces, no infinite loops.
7Eyebrow → Headline → Support, always; wide-tracked uppercase eyebrow at 0.22em.
8Scrims not capsules — type over photo sits on a gradient scrim, never a rounded box.
9Parchment ground for Shal’Artem editorial; cream for Shaltoux; light for Ibucap.
10No emoji in clinical or corporate copy — WhatsApp and social mechanics only.
11Token colours only — var(--*) names in CSS; raw hexes live in the token file alone.
12Flex/grid with gap — never per-element margins between sibling UI.
