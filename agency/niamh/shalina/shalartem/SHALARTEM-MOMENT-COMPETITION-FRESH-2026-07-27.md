## WHY THESE ARE DIFFERENT FROM LAST TIME

The first round leaned on four mechanics that all asked the same thing in different clothes: *make us content about your own moment* — testify, predict, film, tag. That's crowded territory, it's high-friction, and the self-testimony angle quietly drifts toward "how the medicine helped me," which is exactly where a medicine brand cannot go.

Both concepts below move off that spot in opposite directions:

- **Concept 1 turns the camera around** — you don't enter for yourself, you stand up for someone *else's* moment. Warmer, more shareable, and it takes the medicine out of the entry entirely.
- **Concept 2 removes the making** — instead of asking people to produce content, we hand them a finished, on-brand artifact in thirty seconds. Every share looks like Shal'Artem, and nobody has to film a thing.

Neither is a dance challenge — that's the fatigued lane. But both ride the **"Rise on Four" anthem** as their sound, so the campaign still has one voice.

---

## ◆ CONCEPT 1 — "PASS THE MOMENT" · a nomination, not an entry

**The line:** *Don't enter for yourself. Name someone whose moment is coming — and Shal'Artem makes sure they don't miss it.*

**The mechanic.** We ask one question: **"Whose moment can't be missed?"** People nominate a person in their life — a mother about to watch a graduation, a friend opening a stall, a father walking a daughter down the aisle, a son coming home — with a one-line why, sent by comment, DM, or WhatsApp voice note. Each week the brand grants one **"Moment Made":** we fund and film that real person's moment in the Bounce Back gold treatment. The nominator and the person they named both share the win — two people celebrating, both amplifying.

**Why it's the right different.** The old lead asked people to talk about themselves. This asks them to care out loud about someone else — which is a more generous, more Nigerian and more Ghanaian act, and structurally more viral, because every entry comes with a second person attached. It also solves the compliance problem at the root: when you're talking about your sister's graduation, malaria never enters the frame. The moment is the whole story.

**How it carries the brand.** "Never miss *your* moment" becomes "help them not miss *theirs*." The brand is the force that gets people back to what matters — the promise dramatised, never claimed.

**Two countries, two voices.**
- **🇳🇬 NG** — loud and mobile: a public nomination wall, Pidgin, real faces, WhatsApp broadcast of the weekly reveal. NMA-endorsed, prize-competition framing.
- **🇬🇭 GH** — relationship-led: framed as a community *spotlight* rather than a hard prize contest, quieter, carried by family and pharmacist trust. (See flag 5 — GH contest legality decides whether it's a "competition" or a "spotlight" there.)

**What it feeds.** Every nomination is a real, pre-consented, already-cast story — the competition is a casting call and a story bank sourced through affection, not self-promotion. The best become the next films.

## ◆ CONCEPT 2 — "THE MOMENT MAKER" · a tool, not a contest form

**The line:** *Thirty seconds to turn your moment into something worth sharing — and the best ones win.*

**The mechanic.** A single mobile web page (built in our house style — this is exactly what the new design skills are for). You type your moment — *"My moment: my daughter's first day of school"* — optionally add a photo, pick your market's flavour (NG ochre / GH artemisia), and the tool instantly composes a beautiful, animated **"Never Miss Your Moment"** card in the Bounce Back visual language. One tap sends it to WhatsApp status, Instagram, or Facebook. The competition layer: each week the most-loved and jury-picked cards win, building to a grand-prize **"Moment Made."**

**Why it's the right different.** The old mechanics all relied on people making content from scratch — filming, writing, editing — which is high-friction, produces off-brand output, and dies in markets where data is expensive. The Moment Maker gives every entrant a crafted, on-brand artifact in seconds. It guarantees the Shal'Artem look on 100% of the UGC, removes the filming barrier entirely, and is shaped natively for WhatsApp status. It's a tool people *want* to use, not a form they have to fill.

**How it carries the brand.** Every share is a free, perfectly art-directed Bounce Back execution in the audience's own words — the "audience becomes the campaign" goal from the first round, but this time we control the craft. The two-voices strategy is baked into the toggle itself.

**Two countries, two voices.** The market flavour switch *is* the NG-anthem / GH-refrain split — vibrant ochre and hustle for Nigeria, natural artemisia and community for Ghana. NG shares loud on status; GH shares inside family and community groups.

**Build + tech.** Single file, no login, no data capture by default (a compliance feature, not just UX), hosted at `centrefree.com/agency/shalartem/moment-maker`. The "Rise on Four" anthem can play under the card. Fast to ship — it's our format.

**What it feeds.** The typed moments become a searchable, live bank of what real people say matters — a casting mine *and* a market-by-market insight engine showing which moments travel.

## ⚠ PHARMA COMPLIANCE — flagged, not cleared (for Kartik + Shalina regulatory)

Shal'Artem is a medicine. A public competition on a medicine brand carries real duties. Naming them, not signing them off.

1. **No purchase-to-enter.** Entry is a moment — a nomination or a card — never a receipt. Do not tie entry to buying or taking an anti-malarial.
2. **Moments, not malaria.** No soliciting "how Shal'Artem cured me." Concept 1 is naturally clean (you talk about someone else's life event). Concept 2 needs a **text-moderation + claims filter** on user-typed input — this is the operational catch of the tool.
3. **Pharmacovigilance / adverse-event capture.** Any public UGC or comment channel on a medicine brand can surface adverse-event mentions — there is a legal duty to capture and report. A moderation + AE-escalation SOP agreed with Shalina PV is the single gate both concepts hang on. **Do not launch either without it.**
4. **No medical claims** in prompts, tool copy, captions, or reposts. "Never Miss Your Moment" and the moment only — no "fast," "cure," "3 days" as treatment language.
5. **NG / GH split.** NMA (Nigeria) endorsement; **no NMA in Ghana, and confirm GH prize-competition legality** — in GH, Concept 1 likely runs as a "spotlight," not a prize draw. Separate compliance copy per market, never mixed.
6. **Minors & consent.** Moments involving children (first day of school, naming) need entrant consent, age-gating, and a no-health-data rule. Clear T&Cs per market law.
7. **Permits & T&Cs.** Prize competitions may need registration in NG and GH — confirm with client legal.
