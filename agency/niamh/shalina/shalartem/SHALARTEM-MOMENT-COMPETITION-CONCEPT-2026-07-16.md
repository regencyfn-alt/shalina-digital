## THE IDEA IN ONE LINE

The Bounce Back campaign tells eight people's moments. The competition asks the whole country for theirs — **"What's the moment you'll never miss?"** — and turns the audience into the campaign.

Everything Shal'Artem already owns — the tagline, the gold at-stake moment, the first-person testimony — is a fill-in-the-blank the public can complete. We don't invent a competition mechanic; we open the one the brand already runs.

---

## WHY THIS IS THE RIGHT COMPETITION FOR THIS BRAND

1. It extends the paid campaign instead of competing with it — every entry is a free Bounce Back execution in the audience's own words.
2. It stays in the human-truth lane the brand doc mandates: the moment is the hero, the product enables. That keeps us clear of medical-claim territory (see guardrails).
3. It is natively shareable — people share their own graduation, their child's first steps, their match day. The brand rides real emotion, not a manufactured gimmick.
4. It feeds production: the best real moments become the next films. The competition is also a casting call and a story mine.
5. August is a moment-dense month — back-to-school, weddings season, football, harvest-season trading. There is no shortage of moments to anchor weekly themes.

## CONCEPT OPTIONS (pick one lead; they can blend)

### ◆ CONCEPT A — "The Moment I'll Never Miss" · UGC testimony contest *(recommended)*
The public completes the brand's own line about their own life: **"The moment I'll never miss: ___."** Photo or 15s video, their real upcoming or treasured moment. Weekly themes, weekly winners, a grand prize at week six. Best entries get reposted in the brand's gold-testimony treatment; the very best become produced films.
*Why lead: highest brand fit, lowest production cost, doubles as a casting/story engine, purely about moments — safest compliance posture.*

### ◆ CONCEPT B — "Don't Miss a Beat" · appointment/prediction game
A weekly engagement game tied to August's unmissable public moments (football fixtures, cultural dates). Predict/participate each week, never miss a round, climb a leaderboard. Higher volume, lighter emotion.
*Trade-off: more distance from the brand story and from the product — reads as generic engagement bait unless tightly art-directed. Good as a secondary layer under A, weak as the lead.*

### ◆ CONCEPT C — "Bounce Back Stories" · creator/filmmaker challenge
Invite Nigerian/Ghanaian creators to shoot a 15s film in the exact Bounce Back grammar (The Weight → The Turn → The Bounce → The Line). Best film is fully produced and boosted as brand content; creator credited and paid.
*Trade-off: smaller funnel, higher craft, strong for reach through creator audiences. Best run as the week-5/6 "pro tier" on top of A.*

### ◆ CONCEPT D — "Six Weeks of Moments" · tag-and-win sweepstake
Each week the brand posts a moment prompt ("the moment you'd cross the country for"); audience tags the person they'd never miss it with. Every tag is an entry. Lowest friction, highest volume, weakest story depth.
*Trade-off: cheap reach, but it's a sweepstake not a campaign — use as the always-on entry floor beneath A.*

**My recommendation:** run **A as the spine**, fold **D in as the low-friction entry floor** (tag-to-enter alongside submit-your-moment), and hold **C** as the week-5/6 creator crescendo. B only if the client wants a football tie and we can art-direct it hard.

## THE SIX-WEEK ARCHITECTURE (Concept A spine)

A theme a week — each one a different kind of irreplaceable moment, mirroring the eight-film sprint so paid and earned reinforce each other.

| Week | Theme | The prompt | Mirrors film |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Launch** | *First Moments* | "The first time that mattered — a first step, a first day, a first stage." | Son's first steps |
| **2** | *Earned Moments* | "The moment you worked years for." Graduations, qualifications, the stage you crossed. | Graduation |
| **3** | *Hustle Moments* | "The opening day you couldn't miss." The stall, the interview, the deal, the launch. | Market day / Job interview |
| **4** | *Family Moments* | "The gathering that only happens once." Naming, wedding, homecoming. | Baby naming / Wedding |
| **5 · Creator week** | *Stage Moments* | Creator challenge opens (Concept C): shoot your bounce-back in 15s. | Stadium performance |
| **6 · Finale** | *Your Moment* | Open theme + grand-prize week: the single moment you'll never miss. Winners announced. | Match day (finale) |

Each week: one hero prompt post, 2–3 amplifiers, reposts of the best entries in the gold treatment, a midweek "moments so far" gallery, and a Friday winner reveal. Cadence matches the brand's existing Tue/Fri media rhythm.

## ENTRY MECHANIC

- **Submit:** comment, DM, or upload a photo/15s clip of your moment with the line "The moment I'll never miss: ___" and the campaign hashtag.
- **Or tag** (low-friction floor, Concept D): tag the person you'd never miss your moment with. Every tag = one entry.
- **Platforms:** Instagram + Facebook + TikTok (the brand's live three). WhatsApp as a private submission channel for people who won't post publicly — important in these markets.
- **No purchase to enter.** Entry is a moment, not a receipt. (This is a compliance decision, not just a UX one — see guardrails.)

---

## PRIZE LADDER (illustrative — Kartik confirms budget)

- **Weekly (×6):** airtime/data bundles + a branded "moment kit"; the strongest weekly moment gets professionally reshot in the Bounce Back gold treatment and featured.
- **"Moment Made" prize (the hero prize):** the brand funds a winner's actual upcoming moment — the graduation photoshoot, the wedding video, tickets to the match, the market-stall launch kit. This is the prize that IS the campaign: we don't give a generic gadget, we make sure they *don't miss their moment*.
- **Grand prize (week 6):** the biggest "Moment Made," plus the winner's story produced as a flagship film and boosted nationally.

---

## JUDGING

Blended, to protect against vote-brigading: engagement (30%) + brand/creative jury pick for story truth and fit (70%). The jury bias is deliberate — a Bounce Back moment must be *specific and irreplaceable*, and only human judgement catches that. Publish the criteria up front.

## HOW IT PAYS BACK THE CAMPAIGN

- Every entry is a free, on-formula testimony asset.
- The best real moments become produced films — the competition is a casting call and a story bank.
- Six weeks of daily emotional UGC compounds reach with near-zero content cost.
- A national library of "moments" gives the brand a bank of proof it can run for months after.

## ⚠ PHARMA COMPLIANCE GUARDRAILS — flagged, not optional

Shal'Artem is a **medicine**. A public UGC competition on a medicine brand carries real regulatory duties. These are for Kartik and Shalina regulatory to confirm before anything runs — I am naming them, not clearing them.

1. **No purchase-to-enter.** Do not tie entry to buying or consuming an anti-malarial. Proof-of-purchase mechanics on a prescription/OTC medicine are a compliance risk and can read as incentivising drug consumption. Entry must be a *moment*, never a receipt.
2. **Keep entries about moments, not malaria.** Do not solicit "how Shal'Artem cured me" testimonials. The brand carries the clinical truth; the public carries the human truth — same wall the brand doc already draws (§7). This also avoids unverifiable public health claims.
3. **Pharmacovigilance / adverse-event capture.** Any public UGC channel on a medicine brand can surface adverse-event mentions. There is a legal duty to capture and report these. We need a moderation + AE-escalation SOP agreed with Shalina PV before launch. This is the single biggest operational flag — do not launch without it.
4. **No medical claims in prompts, captions, or reposts.** No "fast," "cure," "guaranteed," recovery-time language. "Never Miss Your Moment" and the moment itself only.
5. **NMA (Nigeria) / GMA (Ghana) endorsement per market — do not mix.** Separate mechanics/compliance copy per market.
6. **Minors & consent.** Moments involving children (first steps, naming) need entrant consent and a no-health-data rule. Clear T&Cs, age-gating on entry, data-handling per market law.
7. **T&Cs + promotion permits.** Prize competitions may need registration/permits in NG and GH. Confirm with client legal.
