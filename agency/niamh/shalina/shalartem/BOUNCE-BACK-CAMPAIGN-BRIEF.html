The idea in one line

Three counts down, rise on four. A signature move anyone can do in a doorway, a market, a stadium — the body telling the brand's truth: the fever takes three days; the moment you rise is yours. We don't ask Nigeria to talk about malaria. We ask Nigeria to show us the comeback.

Why dance carries this

The move is the message.

The theme is physical. "Bounce back" is literally a movement — no brand metaphor to explain. Down, down, down, UP.

It's the house's proven format. Street style + user content + micro-influencers is the machine Shalina already runs in Nigeria. We're loading a proven gun with a sharper bullet.

Dance travels where copy can't. A move needs no literacy, no language split, no caption. Lagos to Kano, one grammar.

It keeps us clean. Nobody dances a medical claim. The clinical truth stays in the brand's own endorsed line; the public's contribution is joy, recovery-energy, life resumed — the safest compliance posture available, safer even than testimony.

Sound + move = algorithm. An ownable audio plus a copyable signature move is the one UGC format that reliably compounds on TikTok and Reels in Nigeria — and we own the sound outright.

The signature move · "3 down, rise on 4"

Everyone starts identical. Everyone finishes different.

Counts 1 – 2 – 3: low, heavy, coiled — shoulders drop a beat each count. The weight. The three days.

Count 4 — the RISE: explode up into YOUR move. Shaku, gbese, zanku, a spin, a leap. The first three counts belong to the brand; the fourth count belongs to Nigeria.

Filmable in 15 seconds: three-down-rise-on-four twice, then freestyle out. Judges score the fourth count — originality — which is what turns a challenge into a competition.

The first three counts belong to the brand.
The fourth belongs to Nigeria.

We make the anthem in-house. One 30–45s Afrobeats track with the count baked into the hook — "one, two, three… RISE." Produced by us, so we own it clean — full commercial + TikTok/Meta sound rights, no licensing exposure, no third-party takedown risk. The sound is the campaign's most valuable asset: every use is an impression with the mechanic embedded.

Who's in it

The streets, the crews, the fuse.

Tier 1 · the floorTHE STREETS

UGC floor — everyone. Post your Bounce Back, your fourth count, the sound. Solo, family, workplace, market, church-steps after service. Weekly winners voted in by the public. WhatsApp channel for those who won't post publicly — load-bearing in this market.

Tier 2 · the showTHE CREWS

Registered dance crews (2–8) battle through the run — the craft tier. Crews bring choreography, costume, location craft, and feed the brand's own video content and future films.

Tier 3 · the fuseTHE IGNITERS

15 Nigerian micro-influencers from our own WhatsApp roster seed the move inside the launch 48-hour window and keep the feed busy through the run. Micro over mega, deliberately: comment-section trust and geographic spread. They post their own entries but cannot win — they're pace-setters who set the standard, with paid-partnership disclosure. The prizes stay with the public floor.

The story floor. Every entry caption invites one line — "My moment I'll never miss: ___." The dance carries the reach; the line carries the brand story home and mines the best stories for next season's films.
