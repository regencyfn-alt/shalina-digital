Concept 02 · How it works
Give them the craft, not the homework.
The mechanic
1Open one mobile web page — built in our house style.
2Type your moment: "My moment: my daughter's first day of school." Add a photo if you like.
3Pick your market flavour — NG ochre / GH artemisia.
4Out comes a beautiful animated "Never Miss Your Moment" card. One tap to WhatsApp status.
5Best and most-loved cards win each week — building to a grand-prize Moment Made.
Why it's the right different

The old mechanics made people build content from scratch — film, write, edit. High-friction, off-brand output, and it dies where data is expensive.

The Moment Maker hands every entrant a crafted, on-brand artifact in seconds. It guarantees the Shal'Artem look on 100% of the UGC, removes the filming barrier entirely, and is shaped natively for WhatsApp status. A tool people want to use — not a form they have to fill.
