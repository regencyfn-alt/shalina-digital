Typography

Display — Playfair Display
Never miss your moment.
Body — Source Sans 3
Sincere, credible, warm. The moment is the hero; the product enables. Display type stays roman — italic only for cast role lines.

Voice

Trust before sell. Documentary, not advertising gloss. Real people, real moments, real light.

The gold rule: one highlighted word per headline — the word at stake — in campaign gold. Never two.

The wall: the brand carries the clinical truth; the public carries the human truth. They never swap sides.

Design Laws

Documentary photography only — no illustration, no mascots, no stock gloss.
Square corners on campaign frames — product cards may round at 18px.
No type effects — no shadows, gradients, or outlines on type, ever.
Scrims, not capsules — type over photography sits on a gradient scrim, never a text box.
Parchment ground for editorial — never white, never grey.
Motion is calm — ease-out, 140–420ms, nothing bounces, nothing loops.

Current Campaign

BOUNCE BACK — rise on four.
Nigeria's comeback dance. Not the three days — what you do after them. Four weeks, one move, the fourth count belongs to everyone.
