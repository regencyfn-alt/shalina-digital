# "BOUNCE BACK (RISE ON FOUR)" — Anthem Brief & Lyric

## The job in one line
A 40-second Afrobeats anthem whose hook physically teaches the dance: three counts down, rise on four. Every play of the sound is a rehearsal of the move.

## Production spec
- **Genre:** Afrobeats — log drum + talking drum forward, warm bass, bright guitar chop. Street-party energy, not gloss.
- **Tempo:** 120 BPM (danceable count; one bar = the full 4-count).
- **Length:** 40–45s master (the platform sound) + 15s cut for the challenge loop.
- **The mechanic in the mix:** counts "ONE, TWO, THREE" land as vocal chant with a descending drum figure; "RISE" hits with a full-stop drop-and-lift — one clean talking-drum strike into the beat opening up. The rise must be *feelable* with eyes closed.
- **Voice:** male lead + crowd-gang vocals on the counts (everyone can chant it); female ad-libs welcome. Nigerian artist, Lagos session — authenticity is the brief, not polish.
- **Rights:** full commercial buyout + TikTok/Meta sound-page licensing. Non-negotiable (one takedown kills the engine).
- **NOT in the lyric:** any medicine, illness, cure, "3 days" as treatment language, or brand claim. The count is a dance count. The brand rides the sound page, not the lyric.

## Lyric (draft — producer and artist free to localise; pidgin welcome)

**[Intro — spoken over drums, crowd energy]**
Dem say you fall? No wahala.
Watch what I do with the four...

**[Hook — gang vocals on counts, lead on the rest]**
ONE — carry am low
TWO — bend am slow
THREE — hold your fire, everybody know...
RISE! (eh!) — I bounce back o!
RISE! (eh!) — na my moment o!
One, two, three... RISE!
One, two, three... RISE!

**[Verse — lead]**
Monday put me down but I no dey stay there,
My moment dey call and I swear say I go dey there —
Graduation gown, market stall, my brother's wedding day,

**[Hook out — crowd doubles, ad-libs fly]**
ONE — carry am low
TWO — bend am slow
THREE — everybody low low low...
RISE! — I bounce back o!
RISE! — never miss my moment o!

## Why this works
The hook IS the tutorial. The gang-vocal counts mean any crowd anywhere can perform the sound with zero learning. "I bounce back o / na my moment o" carries both campaign lines without a single claim. The 15s cut starts at the hook — perfect challenge loop.
