Pass the Moment.

Don't enter for yourself. Name someone whose moment is coming — and Shal'Artem makes sure they don't miss it. A nomination, not an entry.

Concept 01 · How it works
One question. One life changed a week.
The mechanic

We ask one thing: "Whose moment can't be missed?" People nominate someone in their life — a mother about to watch a graduation, a friend opening a stall, a father walking a daughter down the aisle — with a one-line why, by comment, DM, or WhatsApp voice note.

Each week the brand grants one "Moment Made": we fund and film that real person's moment in the Bounce Back gold treatment. The nominator and the nominated both share the win — two people celebrating, both amplifying.

Why it's the right different

The last round asked people to talk about themselves — crowded, and it slides toward "how the medicine helped me," where a medicine brand cannot go.

This turns the camera around. Caring out loud about someone else is warmer, more shareable — every entry brings a second person who amplifies — and it solves compliance at the root: when you're talking about your sister's graduation, malaria never enters the frame. The moment is the whole story.

🇳🇬 NG · loud & mobile
A public nomination wall, Pidgin, real faces, WhatsApp broadcast of the weekly reveal. NMA-endorsed, prize-competition framing.
🇬🇭 GH · relationship-led
Framed as a community spotlight, not a hard prize contest — quieter, carried by family and pharmacist trust.

The prize is the point. We don't hand out a gadget — we fund the winner's actual moment: the graduation shoot, the wedding video, the market-stall launch. The prize IS the promise, made real.

What it feeds

Every nomination is a real, pre-consented, already-cast story — the competition doubles as a casting call and a story bank, sourced through affection, not self-promotion. The best become the next films.
