## THE IDEA IN ONE LINE

**THE BOUNCE BACK — Nigeria's comeback dance.** Three counts down, rise on four. A signature move anyone can do in a doorway, a market, a stadium — the body telling the brand's truth: the fever takes three days; the moment you rise is yours. We don't ask Nigeria to talk about malaria. We ask Nigeria to show us the comeback.

---

## WHY DANCE IS THE RIGHT VEHICLE FOR THIS THEME

1. **The theme is physical.** "Bounce back" is literally a movement. No brand metaphor to explain — the move IS the message. Down, down, down, UP.
2. **It's the house's proven format** — street style + UGC + micro-influencers is the machine Shalina already knows how to run in Nigeria. We're loading a proven gun with a sharper bullet.
3. **Dance travels where copy can't.** A move needs no literacy, no language split, no caption. Lagos to Kano, one grammar.
4. **It keeps us clean.** Nobody dances a medical claim. The product's clinical truth stays in the brand's own endorsed line; the public's contribution is joy, recovery-energy, life resumed. The safest compliance posture available — safer even than testimony.
5. **Sound + move = algorithm.** Challenges with an ownable audio and a copyable signature move are the only UGC formats that reliably compound on TikTok/Reels in NG.

---

## THE SIGNATURE MOVE — "3 DOWN, RISE ON 4"

The choreographic core, designed to be learnable in one watch:

- **Counts 1–2–3:** low, heavy, coiled — shoulders drop a beat each count. (The weight. The three days.)
- **Count 4:** the RISE — explode up into YOUR move. The rise is deliberately open: every dancer finishes it their own way. Legwork, shaku, gbese, zanku, a spin, a leap — the first three counts belong to the brand, **the fourth count belongs to Nigeria.**
- Filmable in 15 seconds: 3-down-rise-on-4 twice, then freestyle out.

This is the mechanic that makes it a *competition* and not just a challenge: everyone starts identical, everyone finishes different. Judges score the fourth count.

**The anthem:** commission one 30–45s Afrobeats track ("Bounce Back") with the count baked into the hook — *"one, two, three… RISE."* Built with a Nigerian producer + a name feature if budget allows. The sound is the campaign's most valuable asset: every use is an impression with the mechanic embedded. ## STRUCTURE — SIX WEEKS, THREE TIERS

### Tier 1 · THE STREETS (UGC floor — everyone)
Post your Bounce Back — 15s, the move, your fourth count, the sound, #BounceBackNaija (+ campaign hashtag). Solo, crew, family, workplace, market, church-steps after service. Weekly winners. WhatsApp entry channel for those who won't post publicly (unchanged from v1 — load-bearing in this market).

### Tier 2 · THE CREWS (street-style competition — the show)
Registered dance crews (2–8) battle through weekly themed rounds. This is the content engine — crews bring choreography, costume, location craft. Top crews advance on blended score; finale is a **live Bounce Back Battle** in Lagos (venue/format with Kartik — market square energy, not hotel ballroom; documentary photography law applies to all brand coverage).

### Tier 3 · THE IGNITERS (micro-influencer engine — the fuse)
20–40 Nigerian micro-influencers (10k–200k; dance, comedy, campus, family niches) briefed and paid to seed the move in week 1 and judge/duet entries through the run. Micro over mega, deliberately: comment-section trust, geographic spread across Lagos/Ibadan/Abuja/PH/Kano/Enugu, and a CPM that lets us buy *frequency of demonstration* — the move must be seen done by ordinary bodies, not one celebrity body. One name-feature on the anthem can carry the mega layer if budget allows.

### The story floor (v1's Concept A, folded in)
Every entry caption invites one line: **"My moment I'll never miss: ___."** The dance carries reach; the line carries the brand story home and keeps the casting/story-mine function — best lines get the gold-treatment repost; best stories feed next season's films.
