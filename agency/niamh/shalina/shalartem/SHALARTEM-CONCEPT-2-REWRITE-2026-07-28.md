## WHAT WENT WRONG (one paragraph, then the fix)

The page gave the tool **five jobs at equal weight**: a card maker, a competition, a market-flavour toggle, an insight engine, and a feeder into Concept 1. When everything is the same size, nothing reads as the idea. Concept 1 landed because it is *one sentence you can repeat in the corridor*. Concept 2 wasn't.

**And there's a name collision nobody caught:** the tool was **"The Moment Maker"** and the prize is **"Moment Made."** Two near-identical names in one deck. That alone would muddy any room.

**Fix:** the tool is now **MY MOMENT**. Prize stays **Moment Made**. One idea, one screen, four verbs.

# ◆ CONCEPT 2 — "MY MOMENT"

## Type your moment. Get a beautiful card. Share it. The best ones win.

**What it is.** One page on your phone. You type the moment you don't want to miss — *"my daughter's first day of school"* — and it hands you back a finished, animated Shal'Artem card. One tap to WhatsApp status.

**Why it wins.** Every other competition asks people to *make* something. This one makes it for them. No filming, no editing, no data cost — and **100% of what gets shared is already on brand.**

**How you enter.** Making a card *is* the entry. Best cards each week win; the season builds to one **Moment Made**.

## HOLDING NOTES — for the room's questions, not the pitch

- **Two markets:** the card picks up NG ochre or GH artemisia. It's a setting, not a strategy slide.
- **Build:** single page, our house format, no login, no data capture. Fast to ship.
- **Compliance catch:** user-typed text needs a claims filter and the PV/adverse-event SOP. Same gate as Concept 1 — do not launch either without it.
- **Later, not now:** the typed moments become a casting bank; MY MOMENT can eventually feed Pass the Moment's nominations. **Both cut from the pitch.** Phase-two thinking presented as phase one is exactly what made this confusing.

---

## THE REAL FIX IS TO SHOW IT

This is a tool. Describing a tool is always going to read as clutter — **ten seconds of holding it explains more than the page ever will.** I can have a working MY MOMENT prototype in our house style, live on a phone, on your word. That converts the whole problem from a copy problem into a demo.
