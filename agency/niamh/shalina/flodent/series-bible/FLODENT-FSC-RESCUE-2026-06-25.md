## 3. WHAT TO TELL THE TEAM

The problem with the original script isn't the story — it's the architecture. Story was first, product found its way in wherever it fit. That has to reverse: **product claim first, story built to earn it.** Zimkhita's hesitation to make a difficult call is a real, relatable emotional block. Flodent clearing that block is a real, functional product moment. Build from there.

New scripts in the series should start with: *What block does Flodent clear for Zimkhita this episode?* Answer that, then build the story.
