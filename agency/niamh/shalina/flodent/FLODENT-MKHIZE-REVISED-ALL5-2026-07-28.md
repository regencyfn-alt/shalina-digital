## THE ONE STRUCTURAL FAULT, AND THE FIX

In every v2 episode **Flodent appears in Beat 1 — before the problem exists.** Gogo brushes, *then* the clinic queue. Amahle brushes, *then* the wobble on stage. The product has already happened by the time there's anything to solve, so structurally it cannot be the solution.

**Fix: the product lands between the problem and the outcome.** Not new words — new order.

**And a second call, which we should take to Misbah openly:** toothpaste cannot solve *character*. Gogo's problem is never her mouth — she's fearless, that's the whole woman. Forcing Flodent to fix her makes the brand look ridiculous. So:

| | Role | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Buhle** | **SOLVER** | Colour countdown = brushing duration. A real behaviour problem the product genuinely fixes. |
| **Zimkitha** | **SOLVER** | One brush before dawn, no second chance all day. A real structural problem. |
| **Amahle** | **SOLVER** | Leads from inches away, all afternoon. Closeness is the obstacle. |
| **Gogo** | **WORLD** | She supplies Flodent to the block. Product as artifact of her care, not her cure. |
| **Sibusiso** | **WORLD** | His brush is bravado — character, deliberately punctured. Forcing solver here kills the joke. |

**How to frame it for the client:** *"You asked for the product to visibly drive the outcome. On three of five we do that honestly. On the other two, forcing it would make Flodent look silly — so instead Flodent owns the world those two live in."*

**Compliance floor, all five:** freshness · brushing duration · whitening only if on-pack. **Never** gums, sensitivity, decay, or "repair" unless it is the registered Triple Action pack claim (still unconfirmed — see EP04).

# EP01 · BUHLE — THE COLOUR COUNTDOWN
**ROLE: SOLVER · the strongest one we have — barely moves**

**The problem Flodent solves:** a six-year-old brushes for three seconds and stops. She has no way of knowing when *done* is.

## The story
Show-and-tell morning, and Buhle's first tooth is wobbling — an enormous thing when you're six. She inspects it in the kettle, the window, the back of a spoon, too scared to touch it and too fascinated to leave it. She won't stay still long enough for anything, least of all brushing; three furious seconds and she's off.

**Then the rule takes over.** Flodent Kids starts bright and fades as she brushes — *brush until the colour is gone, and when it's gone you're done.* She can't rush it and she can't cheat it. So for the first time all morning she stands still and watches the mirror, and the colour clears on the paste's clock, not hers. In the quiet after, with no drama, the tooth frees in the foam.

Second test at the door: will the class think the gap looks silly? One mirror-check, one more grin. It holds — she's already done the waiting once today. She wears the gap like a medal, and that evening tucks the tooth under her pillow while the whole house watches from the doorway.

**What changed:** the countdown now *causes* the stillness that frees the tooth, instead of sitting beside it. Same story, product load-bearing.

# EP03 · ZIMKITHA — THE FIXER'S DAY
**ROLE: SOLVER**

**The problem Flodent solves:** she gets one brush, pre-dawn, and the day never gives her a second. By afternoon most people would start keeping their distance — and her whole day is close-up.

## The story
Pre-dawn, and the day is already stacking against her — a lost shoe, a leak under the sink, everyone late. She brushes once, in the dark, because she knows exactly what the day is: this is the only chance she'll get.

**And the day proves it.** No pause, no bathroom, no second brush. A teacher pulled aside at the gate. A neighbour's crisis on the pavement, head to head. The toddler on her hip. Every fix she makes today, she makes at arm's length or closer — and **Flodent Original's freshness is still with her at four in the afternoon**, so she never once leans back from anyone. She doesn't think about it. That's the point: she has enough to think about.

Evening turns it over. She walks in braced for the last shift and finds the fixing already done — the family has cooked, the load lifted for once. She laughs so she won't cry.

**What changed:** the morning brush is no longer "her one still second" (self-care, no product job). It's now the only brush she gets, and lasting freshness is what carries her through a day made entirely of close conversations.

# EP05 · AMAHLE — SMILE CHECKED, VOICE CHECKED
**ROLE: SOLVER**

**The problem Flodent solves:** she's leading from inches away, for hours, with no break to brush.

## The story
The day Amahle leads for the first time — front and centre, the younger ones on her count instead of the other way round. Nerves of the teenage kind that live in the mirror. She brushes before the hall, the family ritual, tube in shot.

**Then the afternoon tests it properly.** Leading isn't standing at the front — it's crouching down to a nine-year-old's height and counting her in, face to face, over and over, for three hours. No break, no bathroom, no second brush. **The freshness holds, so she never pulls back mid-count** — and the little ones stay right where they can see her.

On stage it almost comes apart: a wobble, the line nearly breaking, seconds to save it. She carries them through — not flawless, fully herself. Home that evening she's teaching Buhle the first eight-count in the kitchen while the family watches from the doorway.

**What changed:** the ritual no longer produces confidence out of thin air. The obstacle is *proximity*, and freshness is what lets her stay in it.

# EP02 · GOGO NOMALANGA — IMBOKODO
**ROLE: WORLD · Flodent is what she gives out, not what fixes her**

**No solver claim. Deliberately.** Gogo has never been short of courage and the audience knows it. The honest placement is that **she's the one who supplies Flodent to the block.**

## The story
Women's Month, and Gogo is up before the street that needs her. **On the shelf by her door: the row of small things she restocks for everyone — including the Flodent.** She takes one for her bag on the way out, the way she takes everything for other people.

Straight into the first fire: the clinic queue is chaos and an elder is about to be turned away. She reorders the whole queue with a smile and one true word nobody else would say, and the elder is seen. **Behind her at the corner, the Flodent billboard she passes every day of her life.**

By midday the street brings her the thing nobody wants to touch — a young one in real trouble, a dispute about to boil. No filter, all sun, she lands the truth wrapped in that smile and the block moves because she moved through it. *Wathint' imbokodo.* **On the way home she leaves one of the small things from her bag with a family that needed it.** No line about it. She just does.

Evening on the stoep, holding court, telling the day back bigger than it was.

**Two placements, no dialogue, no claim:** the shelf she restocks, the billboard on her corner. The brand is an artifact of her care.

# EP04 · SIBUSISO — MAN OF THE HOUSE
**ROLE: WORLD · the brush is character, and it's supposed to be wrong**

**No solver claim.** The joke is that his confidence is unearned. If Flodent *caused* the confidence, the day couldn't puncture it — and we'd lose the funniest episode in the set.

## The story
The women are out owning their day, so Sibusiso is home to handle things. How hard can it be. The morning brush comes with full bravado, tube in shot, the speech he actually believes: *clear head, clear win, today is mine.*

**Then the day starts teaching him.** The shelf fights back. The mower goes to war with the flowerbed. He wrestles one thing into half-working order and the confidence comes roaring back, entirely unearned. Emboldened, he takes on lunch and the washing at once — the toddler negotiation is lost outright, the chaos compounds, and then the worst clock of all: headlights in the drive.

They find the beautiful wreckage of his effort, and something new in his eyes. **Later, the family tube back on the bathroom shelf where it lives — one shelf, one household, everybody's.** The honouring is done by laughter, not a lecture.

**⚠ ONE OPEN ITEM:** if Sibusiso carries **Triple Action**, the *"repair"* claim in that pack lockup is unconfirmed. **Confirm it's on-pack before this one ships** — it's a harder problem than anything else in the set, because it sits in the product name.
