## THE STORY (30 seconds)

Sipho Mkhize has been dreading this morning for a month. In the little workshop behind the house sits his father's toolbox — dented green steel, older than the democracy, the thing he learned his trade from. And outside the workshop, every morning at seven, waits Thabo: nineteen, patient, good hands, the apprentice who is ready — and both of them know it. Today the first solo job comes in. Today Sipho either keeps the toolbox, or passes it on.

He stands in the doorway in the half-dark looking at the box. From inside the house, faintly, Gudi's guitar — the boy plays every morning now, since the scholarship. Life moved forward for everyone. That's the problem with fresh starts: eventually it's your turn to give the one that costs you.

Bathroom. Morning light. Sipho reaches for the Flodent, and brushes — slow, deliberate, a man putting himself in order. He looks at himself in the mirror the way Zimkhita looked at herself in Ep01. The freshness lands. *Today is the day.*

He walks the toolbox out across the yard. Thabo straightens up, wipes his hands, doesn't dare assume. Sipho sets the box down in front of him, flips the latch open once — checking it's all there, the last inspection — and steps back. One nod. That's the whole speech.

The turn is Thabo's face: disbelief, then weight, then standing taller. He carries the box to the waiting car and gets to work — and Sipho watches from the workshop door with his coffee, exactly where Zimkhita stood in Ep01, watching. The givers don't need credit. Packshot. Flodent. Fresh Start.
