The Mkhizes — August
Structure v2 · Four-Beat, Two-Round

Client revision: every story now carries all four beats, run twice.

The four beats — every story, every round:

1 Relate — we see ourselves2 Conflict — a real problem3 Time — a ticking clock that raises the stakes4 Resolution — the payoff

The second round: a 30-second film runs the four-beat arc twice. Round 1 (0-15s) is a first, smaller conflict resolved at the morning brush; Round 2 (15-30s) escalates to a bigger conflict and the final resolution with the brand endline. Four beats per 15-second round, eight beats across the film.

On the Flodent engine: the brush is the Round 1 Time pivot (product in use, the permissioned space, no line spoken over it). The brand lands in-world at each round's Resolution - the every-fourth-beat law - and the claim is held to the very last frame.

'Time' read: taken as the ticking-clock / countdown that raised the stakes in Buhle's story (the colour countdown) - now built into all five. Endline: packshot line still pending your ruling (series "Every day is a fresh start" vs the locked Template-001 "Grab Life, Grab the Freshness!").

Episode 01

The Colour Countdown

Buhle · youngest · 6-7 · The Inspector

Round 1. Relate: show-and-tell morning, Buhle's first tooth is wobbling - every child knows the feeling. Conflict: she is too scared to touch it and can't settle. Time: the Flodent Kids colour countdown - brush until the bright colour fades to nothing. Resolution: the colour clears and the loose tooth frees in the foam, the brave gap-grin arrives.

Round 2. Relate: she wants to show the whole world, but it is nearly time to leave. Conflict: a flicker of new doubt - will the gap look silly to the class? Time: one last mirror-check before the door, the clock ticking to school. Resolution: she owns the gap-grin and wears it like a medal all day; that night the tooth goes under the pillow, the family proud.

The Fixer's Day

Zimkitha · 38 · Mom · The Fixer

Round 1. Relate: pre-dawn, the day's demands already stacking. Conflict: a morning crisis - a lost shoe, a leak, everyone running late, all of it hers to catch. Time: her one still breath at the brush, then the clock - school starts in minutes. Resolution: she solves it and gets them out the door, still smiling big.

Round 2. Relate: now her own day - the job, a neighbour, the errands, the school run twice. Conflict: stretched too thin, something of hers about to slip, and she never asks for help. Time: racing the last of the day home before it all catches up. Resolution: she walks in and the fixing is already done - the family cooked, the load lifted; the fixer, for once, fixed.
