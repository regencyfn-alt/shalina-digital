## 1. The Big Idea

The family as episodic, authentic entertainment and brand ambassadors. Addictive short films and dynamic post moments. Each episode starts with one family member brushing their teeth — **product in the image, used, never placed as a product shot** — then the camera follows that character through a fast-edited day, returning home in the evening, where there is love and caring.

**Theme: Strong teeth. Real life. · Every day is a fresh start.**

The narrative lives only where there is an engaging format. Ads are blocked by default; stories are not.

## 2. The Permissioned Space (Shane's law, verbatim in substance)

The brushing scene is always a **natural teeth-brushing scene — our permissioned space in their private lives.** We are guests in the Mkhize bathroom every morning because the story earned the invitation. Nothing in that scene ever performs at the audience: no label-to-camera, no held pose, no claim spoken. The character brushes because it's morning and they have a day to start.

## 4. The Story Law (document workflow — every episode, every brand)

1. **Story first.** Told simply, as fluid continuous prose — an intro, a middle, an outro. No jargon, no shot language. A person reads it and feels it.
2. Then: **why the audience will love it**, and **why the brand benefits.** Three sections, that's the whole first movement.
3. **SIGN-OFF.** Nothing proceeds without it.
4. **Storyboard after sign-off, in the same document:** images plus short scene descriptions.
5. **SIGN-OFF again.**
6. **Then story beats and cuts** — the timed production table.
7. The complete document goes to a staff designer, or stays Niamh's baby. Either way it must carry a fluid, continuous story with intro, outro, and **correct brand placement at every fourth beat plus any other natural opportunity.**
8. **Deliverable format: PDF.** The client computes PDF; everything client-facing ships as PDF.

## 5. Brand Placement Rules

- The brush scene is the anchor placement (product in use, natural).
- Every fourth beat thereafter, the brand appears **in the world**: a bag, a counter, a shelf, a passing billboard — immersive, never inserted.
- Claim lives at the end frame only: **"Every day is a fresh start."**
- The product never triggers the story like a magic object; it accompanies the person who acts.

## 6. The Four Pillars

1. **Family Values / Authenticity** — a real family in a real scenario, ongoing. The family lives the brand; the brand never performs at them.
2. **Engagement / Storytelling** — episodic, fast-paced, built for YouTube and TikTok; designed to be watched, shared, remembered.
3. **Immersive** — the brand sits inside the world: shelves, bags, in passing.
4. **Start Fresh** — families with values who flourish despite challenges and put community first. Resilience is the throughline.

## 7. Tone & Voice

New. Loud. Alive. Not therapy, not gentle. Confidence speech: *I am confident. I walk in like I belong here. Today is mine to take. Clear breath, clear head, clear win. Every morning resets the score.* Warmth is earned, never syrupy; the family teases, Gogo has no filter, nobody lectures.

## 8. Format & Cadence (FY26/27 plan)

- Hero episodes: **40 seconds**, character-led. Moment posts: **10 seconds**.
- SA anthem market: TikTok-led, 2 posts/week baseline. Angola refrain market: Meta-anchored, 1/week, PT-first.
- 100% WhatsApp replication — the owned community is the distribution spine.

## 9. Palette (client system — all Flodent deliverables)

Crimson Earned `#E31F26` leads (headlines, CTA, mark) · Flodent Blue `#0A6EBD` lifts · Light Blue `#4FB3E8` accents · Pearl `#FAF6F0` surfaces (never pure white) · Charcoal `#1A1A1A` body (never black). **No greens, no teals, ever.**

## 10. Engine Derivation (Universal Beat Bible v1.0)

Fantasy dial: **0% — documentary.** Witness principle: the family watches the family (the doorway pose is the series signature). Five beats map: Question (the character's moment approaching) → Ordinary (real home texture) → Turn (the natural brush — the permissioned space) → Proof (the character steps into the day) → Return (home in the evening; love and caring; end frame). All five universal tests apply to every script, plus the placement law (§5).
