## Creative framework (the imagination concept)
The defining device across all eight videos is fantasy — the world as a child sees it. Adults perform the Germol rituals with quiet confidence; children see the magic. When Germol ASL hits the water, it glows. When Soap lathers, a protective dome rises. Germ-creatures scatter. Force fields appear on door handles. Caregivers transform into shield-bearing protectors.

Not surrealism, not comedy — the earnest, sincere imagination of a child who has grown up where Germol is daily ritual and has correctly understood what it means, even without the science. The magic is real. The protection is real. The child can see it.

Structure, every video: **Shot 1 (0–10s)** reality, a family moment. **Shot 2 (10–20s)** the fantasy break — the child's eyes take over. **Shot 3 (20–30s)** return to reality carrying the emotional weight. Product hero. "Shields with Love and Care."

## ASL Treatment 1 — Hands That Teach (2 Jul · Family Trust)
*"Some lessons aren't taught with words." #GermolShieldsWithLove*
Winter morning, real SA home, concrete laundry basin, net curtain. S1: tight on a child's wide eyes watching completely; reveal teen sibling at the basin pouring Germol ASL — amber liquid in pale light, a ritual owned. S2: as amber hits water the basin transforms — deep electric Germol Blue glow pulsing outward in ripples, edges shifting to Safety Green; comic germ-creatures flee the light; the teen's hands move through the glow unbothered — they've forgotten it's extraordinary, only the child still knows. S3: fantasy dissolves; the teen notices the child and gestures them over; two sets of hands in the water; the child looks up, the teen gives a small serious nod — *you know how to do this now*. Product hero on basin edge. The inheritance is complete.
**Why it works:** school holidays make the who-teaches-care question live; the fantasy honours the child's intelligence (their instinct that the ritual is significant is CORRECT); the beckon in S3 is the free emotional payload; S2 is the scroll-stop, S3 is the share.

## ASL Treatment 2 — The Daily Shield (6 Jul · Family Trust)
*"Winter doesn't wait, and neither do we." #GermolASL #WinterReady*
Early, house not awake. Working mother, doek and house coat, fills spray bottle from the ASL cap by muscle memory. S1: child (~5) appears at doorway level, watching the watcher; mother focused, unaware. S2: each surface she wipes glows with a blue shield imprint, Germol Blue radiating from every touch point, a grid of protection building room by room. She does not glow — she is not a cartoon; she is the person who makes the house safe before the family wakes. The child sees exactly what she is doing. S3: she turns, sees the child, gives the quiet smile that means *I see you*. Child places a small hand on the wiped door handle, looks at hand, then mother. The house is safe; they made it safe together. Product hero.
**Why it works:** the working mother 25–45 is the purchase decision-maker; this affirms rather than adds to her worry — her unwitnessed routine is protection a child sees as magic (profound, and true); Ms. Germol present in spirit without casting — any real woman doing the routine becomes her → scales to UGC and influencer iterations; every mother knows she has been watched this way — that memory is the share.

## ASL Treatment 3 — Winter-Proof Home (16 Jul · Winter Resilience)
*"Winter keeps us indoors — Germol ASL keeps our homes protected." #WinterProofHome*
Mid-winter indoor day, curtains against the grey. Mother does the surfaces, methodically: counter, door handle, light switch, tap. A child (~4) follows with their own pretend cloth — the apprenticeship in its most everyday form. S2 (fantasy builds gradually): each wiped surface keeps a faint blue shield impression like a ghostly handprint, steadying into permanence; the home fills surface by surface until the whole house is mapped in blue Germol light — a force-field house; the child stops pretending and turns slowly in the middle of the protected world. S3: child touches a shielded handle, holds it, then leads the mother's hand onto it with theirs — trying to give her the experience they are having. She can't feel it; she smiles anyway, because she knows what they mean: the house is safe and she made it safe. Product hero.
**Why it works:** Winter Resilience speaks to the anxious practical heart of the audience — indoor season, surface contact, real concern answered without fear language or germ close-ups; the grid is the most cinematic image of the series (Marvel/DC visual language applied to the humble and real); the child leading the mother's hand is the most emotionally precise moment of the eight.

## ASL Treatment 4 — Back on the Pitch (23 Jul · Football Buzz)
*"A little graze never stopped a champion." #BafanaBafana #BackOnThePitch*
Township pitch, golden late-winter light. Teen player mistimes a tackle, goes down, grass graze on the knee — stinging, real. At the fence: a child (~6), face pressed to the wire. S1: close on the player feeling it; an adult crouches with kit bag — Germol ASL bottle and cotton wool, quick practiced application; the child grips the wire. S2: in the child's mind the application is an ARMORING — blue light spreads from the graze, covers leg then body, a Germol Blue energy field that pulses once and holds; germ-creatures scatter; the player stands restored to full power. S3: reality — the player receives, moves, scores clean; the child erupts, both arms up: they watched the armor go on, they KNEW. Product hero on football-gold gradient.
**Why it works:** July is peak SA football fervour, Bafana content over-performs on TikTok; the brand is already inside the real moment (sideline first-aid is lived, not staged); the child carries the fantasy, the hope, and the release — the player's story is the event, the child's experience is the video; the celebration is because the magic worked — the strongest product claim, said wordlessly by a child.
