## Logo
- Clear space: 1× the height/width of the 'o' in the logo — no text or graphics inside the exclusion zone.
- Minimum size: 0.75in (1.9cm) print · 80px digital.
- Three colour versions (darker background / white background variants).
- DON'Ts: no recolouring, no rotation, no shadow, no outline, no changing the shape of the white rectangle, no placement on unsuitable backgrounds.
- Sub-line tailors to product: Hygiene Soap / Anti-Septic Liquid / Wet Wipes.

## Type
- One font package: **RUBIK BOLD** (full weights/styles).
- Campaign fonts may change; **logo and sign-off fonts never change**.
- Sign-off lines: Sentence Case. Single words, short statements, CTAs, annotations may be UPPERCASE for impact.
- Alignment: left-aligned standard; centre selectively (call-outs, headlines, quotes); **never right-aligned**.
