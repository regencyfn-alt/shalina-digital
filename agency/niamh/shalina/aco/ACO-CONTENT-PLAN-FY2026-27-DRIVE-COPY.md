## Campaign frame
- **Campaign idea:** One jar of ACO Pine Gel does the work of a whole cupboard. The line carries the whole strategy — save money, save time, multipurpose.
- **Owned line:** "You Only Need One." (PT: "Só Precisa de Um.")
- **Lead market:** Angola (Portuguese) · Also: Nigeria, Ghana, Francophone Africa
- **Phases:** P1 LAUNCH 19–29 Jun 2026 (hero film + product + first proof) · P2 SUSTAIN 1–18 Jul 2026 (daily rolling calendar: prove, educate, build trust, localise) · P3 AMPLIFY Aug 2026 proposed (offers, UGC, top-uses — extension, confirm budget/flight)
- **Channels:** Meta (FB + IG Reels/Stories), TikTok, YouTube (film + cutdowns), WhatsApp (status + CTA destination)
- **Layers:** Hero (emotional spine) · Product (value + mechanic) · Proof (Test Zone demos)
- **Brand law:** the ACO pack is never re-lettered — every appearance uses the real packshot.
- Workbook tabs on Drive: Launch Schedule · Video Assets · Static Assets · Sustain Calendar (Jul) · Amplify (Aug) · CTA Bank · Channels & Cadence · KPIs & Measurement.
