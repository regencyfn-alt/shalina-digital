Story Brief
The Story
Two stovetops.
One winner.

Glória and Ana receive the same challenge: a stovetop caked in heavy cooking grease. Glória reaches for her arsenal of specialised products and scrubs hard. Ana measures one ACO tub at 1:1 and clears the surface in a single pass — teaching the audience the right ratio as they watch it work.

Why It Works · Audience
The ratio is the hook.

Every household has a version of Glória's cleaning frustration. The scroll stops at Um para um — a piece of practical knowledge delivered inside a story that already has their attention. They leave having learned something, not just having seen something.

Why It Works · Brand
"Só Precisas de Um" under pressure.

Heavy grease is the hardest case. If one tub clears it in a single pass, it clears everything. This story proves the campaign truth — not by claiming it, but by showing it happen in real time, on the hardest surface in the kitchen.

1:1
Education Moment · Embedded in Scene 02
Gel to Water — Heavy Grease Ratio
Ana holds the bowl to camera and names it: "Um para um — gordura pesada." This is the dilution ratio for heavy grease. It is said once, clearly, while the audience is already watching. It becomes product knowledge without becoming a tutorial.

Characters
👗
Glória
The Contrast · Late 30s · Luanda
Who She Is Organised, capable, proud of her home. She buys what the market tells her to buy — a different product for every surface. She is not foolish. She is just not yet Ana.
Look Long straight hair, gold-burgundy wax kaftan. Warm, funny, relatable. Energy: busy, capable, slightly harried.
In This Story Switches between degreaser spray, dish soap, and multi-surface foam. Scrubs hard. The grease resists. She is not defeated — she is still fighting when Ana is already done.
⚠ Never make her a fool. She is the audience. Most of them are Glória.
🌿
Ana
The Hero · Early 30s · Luanda
Who She Is Moves with quiet confidence. She figured something out and it shows — not smugly, just cleanly. She carries ACO the way someone carries a thing that actually works.
Look Natural afro, teal-gold wax dress. Clean lines, natural ease. Less friction in her environment than in Glória's.
In This Story Sets out one ACO tub. Pours 1:1, holds it up, names the ratio. One cloth, one pass. Doesn't look at Glória. Looks at us.
⚠ Never make her cold or superior. She is where Glória is going. She's sharing, not winning.
