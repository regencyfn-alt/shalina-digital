# ACO Pine Gel — Story 01: The Cupboard
### Creative Storyboard · English Version
**Campaign:** "You Only Need One." · **Client:** Shalina Healthcare · **Market:** Angola  
**Format:** 40-second social film · 4 scenes × 10 sec · TikTok / Instagram Reels · 9:16 vertical

---

## The Story in One Line

> A woman buried under a cupboard full of cleaning products discovers her neighbour gets the same job done with a single green tub.

---

## Emotional Arc

| Stage | Audience Feeling | Trigger |
|-------|-----------------|---------|
| **Recognition** | "That's me. I have that cupboard." | Gloria's chaos |
| **Curiosity** | "Wait — she only has one thing?" | Ana's reveal |
| **Belief** | "OK, I saw it work on everything." | The proof |
| **Desire** | "I want my cupboard to look like Ana's." | End slate |

---

## Characters

### GLORIA — The Contrast
- Late 30s · Angolan woman
- Long straight hair, gold-burgundy wax kaftan
- Warm, funny, relatable — she is us, not the villain
- **Appears in:** Scene 01

### ANA — The Hero
- Early 30s · Angolan woman
- Natural afro, teal-gold wax dress
- Quiet confidence — she knows the secret, she's sharing it
- **Appears in:** Scenes 02 & 03

---

## Scene Breakdown

---

### SCENE 01 · The Problem `0:00 – 0:10`

**Setting:** Gloria's kitchen, warm afternoon light

| Beat | Timecode | Action |
|------|----------|--------|
| **Open** | 0:00 | Gloria's kitchen. Cupboard door slightly ajar — products already threatening to spill. |
| **Cascade** | 0:03 | She nudges the door. Everything falls: mop bucket, bleach, floor spray, stain remover, glass cleaner, scourer. Arms full, face incredulous. |
| **Beat to camera** | 0:07 | Arms still overflowing, she looks directly at us. The look says: *"This is my life."* Slight shake of the head. Funny. Real. |

**Voice Over:**
> "Wait — how many cleaners does one home actually need?"

**Director's Note:** Play warm, not slapstick. The humour comes from recognition, not mockery. Hand-held. Medium shot tightens to close on her face.

---

### SCENE 02 · The Reveal `0:10 – 0:20`

**Setting:** Ana's kitchen, bright and ordered

| Beat | Timecode | Action |
|------|----------|--------|
| **Inside the cupboard** | 0:10 | Hard cut. Camera is inside looking out. One green ACO Pine Gel tub sits centred on the shelf. Nothing else. |
| **Pull back** | 0:13 | Reveal Ana holding the tub at chest height. Quiet confidence. She already knows. |
| **One finger up** | 0:16 | She raises one index finger toward camera — *"this one."* Her smile breaks open. Wide. Warm. She's sharing the secret. |

**Voice Over:**
> "Meet ACO Pine Gel. The only cleaner your home needs."

**Director's Note:** Linger on the inside-cupboard shot before the reveal. Ana is aspirational but accessible — warm wisdom, not superiority. Label must read clearly.

---

### SCENE 03 · The Proof `0:20 – 0:30`

**Setting:** Kitchen, bathroom, floor — same green tub throughout

| Beat | Timecode | Action |
|------|----------|--------|
| **Kitchen hob** | 0:20 | ACO tub on counter. Ana pours onto a cloth, wipes the hob. One stroke. It gleams. Effortless. |
| **Bathroom sink** | 0:23 | Same tub, beside the basin. She scrubs, the grime lifts. Small satisfied nod. It sparkles. |
| **Floor** | 0:27 | Wide shot. Ana mops. Tub visible in corner of frame. She doesn't slow down. Three rooms. One product. Case closed. |

**Voice Over:**
> "Kitchen. Bathroom. Floors. One tub — every surface in your home."

**Director's Note:** ACO tub visible in every cut. Each location is exactly 3 seconds. Pace builds slightly. Product does the talking.

---

### SCENE 04 · End Slate `0:30 – 0:40`

**Setting:** Clean white — no location, no character

| Beat | Timecode | Action |
|------|----------|--------|
| **Cut to white** | 0:30 | Pure, breathing. ACO Pine Gel tub rises in from the bottom, centred. Label fills 40% of frame. |
| **Tagline** | 0:33 | "YOU ONLY NEED ONE." builds in — word by word or all at once. Declarative. Done. |
| **Logo settles** | 0:36 | ACO red logo, bottom right. "All Purpose Pine Gel Cleaner · Angola" underneath. Holds 4 full seconds. |

**Super / Text on Screen:**
> YOU ONLY NEED ONE.  
> ACO Pine Gel · All Purpose Pine Gel Cleaner

**Director's Note:** No music swell. Silence gives the tagline room. If audio is used — one clean piano note or a breath of wind. The white is a full stop.
