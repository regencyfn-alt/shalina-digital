# ACO Angola — Story 01: O Armário
### *The Cupboard*

**Brand:** ACO Multipurpose Household Gel  
**Market:** Angola · Portuguese  
**Format:** 4 × 10-second scenes · Social/TikTok/IG  
**Characters:** Glória · Ana  
**Status:** Beat board — draft  
**Date:** 2026-06-29

---

## CHARACTER BIBLES

### GLÓRIA
**Who she is:** Late 30s. Luanda. Organised, capable, proud of her home. She buys what the market tells her to buy — a different product for every surface, because that's how it's always been done. She is not foolish. She is just not yet Ana.  
**Look:** Warm-toned skin, natural hair tied back for cleaning day. Practical home clothes. Energy: busy, slightly harried, capable.  
**Never:** Make her a fool. She is the audience — most of them are Glória. She becomes curious, not humiliated.  
**Preservation line:** *Preserve Glória's warm, capable, relatable presence — do not make her look stressed or incompetent.*

### ANA
**Who she is:** Early 30s. Luanda. Moves with quiet confidence. She figured something out and it shows — not smugly, just cleanly. She carries ACO the way someone carries a thing that actually works.  
**Look:** Clean lines, natural ease. Same Angolan domestic setting but lighter somehow. Less friction.  
**Never:** Make her superior or cold. She is where Glória is going.  
**Preservation line:** *Preserve Ana's easy confidence — warm, not superior; she has a small piece of knowledge and it makes everything simpler.*

## SCENE BREAKDOWN — 4 × 10 SECONDS

---

### SCENE 1 — O ARMÁRIO DA GLÓRIA [0–10s]
*Glória's Cupboard*

Glória's kitchen. Morning light. She opens the cleaning cupboard — it is full. Floor cleaner. Bathroom spray. Glass cleaner. Tile cleaner. Degreaser. She pulls them out one by one and lines them up on the counter. It takes two trips. She looks at the row of bottles. This is just how it is.

**Visual logic:** The counter fills up. Product after product. The sheer number of them is the joke — not mean, just honest.  
**Camera:** Medium on cupboard opening → wide on counter as bottles accumulate → close on her face: not annoyed, just ready to work.  
**VO:** *(none — let the image speak)*  
**Audio:** Kitchen sounds. Cabinet door. Bottles landing on counter, one by one.

---

### SCENE 2 — O ARMÁRIO DA ANA [10–20s]
*Ana's Cupboard*

Ana. Same morning, different home. She opens her cleaning cupboard. One bottle of ACO. She picks it up. Closes the cupboard. That's it. She's already walking.

**Visual logic:** The contrast is everything. The cupboard behind her is almost empty — and that is the point. It isn't a lack; it's a choice.  
**Camera:** Same framing as Scene 1 — medium on cupboard opening → the reveal: one bottle → wide on Ana already moving, bottle in hand.  
**VO:** *(none — the image earns it)*  
**Audio:** Cabinet door. Single tap of the bottle on the shelf. Footsteps.

---

### SCENE 3 — ACO EM ACÇÃO [20–30s]
*ACO in Action*

Ana moves through her home. Kitchen counter — ACO. Bathroom tiles — ACO. Glass window — ACO. One bottle, three surfaces, no hesitation. Quick cuts. Each surface comes clean. She doesn't put the bottle down.

**Visual logic:** The product demonstration that doesn't feel like a demonstration. She's just cleaning her house, and it happens to work on everything.  
**Camera:** Three tight cuts — each surface + hands + ACO bottle visible in frame. Fast, confident, rhythmic. The same bottle in each shot = the lock.  
**VO:** *"Cozinha. Casa de banho. Janelas."* — *Kitchen. Bathroom. Windows.*  
**Audio:** Clean spray sounds. Surface being wiped. Light, satisfying.

---

### SCENE 4 — RESOLUÇÃO [30–40s]
*Brand Resolution*

Glória looks at her counter of products. Looks at Ana's single bottle (or a cut to the ACO pack as hero). Something lands — not embarrassment, just recognition. A small smile. She gets it now.

Cut to: ACO Multipurpose Household Gel. Clean white surface. The bottle, confident.

**Visual logic:** The emotional close. Glória's recognition is the audience's recognition. The packshot is earned.  
**Camera:** Close on Glória's face — the click of understanding → cut to product hero shot.  
**VO:** *"ACO. Só precisa de um."*  
**Pack:** ACO Multipurpose Household Gel bottle. Full product visible.  
**Endline:** *"ACO. Só precisa de um."* — ACO. You Only Need One.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Colour/look:** Angolan domestic interior. Warm light. Real homes, not studio-clean. The difference between Glória's space (slightly cluttered with products) and Ana's (clear) should be felt, not stated.

**No dialogue in Scenes 1–2.** The contrast between the cupboards carries everything. Don't fill it with words.

**Scene 3 VO is optional** — if the visuals are strong enough, cut it. Test both.

**Glória's recognition in Scene 4** is the emotional hinge of the whole series. Every episode ends with her getting it. Protect that moment — it's the serotonin hit.
