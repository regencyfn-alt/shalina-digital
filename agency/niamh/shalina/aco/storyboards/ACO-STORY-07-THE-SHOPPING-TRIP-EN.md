# ACO Pine Gel — Story 07: The Shopping Trip
### Creative Storyboard · English Version
**Campaign:** "You Only Need One." · **Client:** Shalina Healthcare · **Market:** Angola  
**Format:** 60-second social film · 4 scenes · TikTok / Instagram Reels · 9:16 vertical

---

## What This Story Is About

The Shopping Trip puts the ACO proposition exactly where it belongs — at the point of purchase. Two women, same supermarket aisle, same cleaning problem to solve. Gloria buys what most people buy: a different product for every surface, a trolley full of spend. Ana buys one green tub and keeps moving.

The film doesn't explain the difference. It shows it. By the time the two women meet at the checkout and exchange six words between them, the audience has already worked it out. That moment — *"That's all you're buying?" / "That's all I need."* — lands because the film has already earned it visually.

**Why it works for ACO:** The supermarket aisle is the exact moment of truth for this brand. This is where the buying decision happens, where competing products sit side by side, and where a confident woman reaching past five options to pick one says more than any claim on a label. The film captures that clarity and makes it aspirational. You don't feel like you're being sold to. You feel like you're being let in on something.

## Scene Breakdown

---

### SCENE 01 · The Aisle `0:00 – 0:20`

**Setting:** Supermarket cleaning aisle, Angola. Bright overhead lighting. Well-stocked shelves. A familiar weekend shop.

| Beat | Timecode | Action |
|------|----------|--------|
| **Open on Gloria** | 0:00 | Gloria pushes her trolley slowly down the cleaning aisle, studying the shelves with the seriousness of someone making important decisions. Already in the trolley: a floor cleaner, a bathroom spray, a glass cleaner. |
| **Adding more** | 0:05 | She picks up a stain remover, reads the back, adds it. Picks up a separate toilet cleaner. Adds it too. The trolley is filling up. Her face is focused but a little exhausted — this happens every month. |
| **Ana enters** | 0:10 | Ana walks into the same aisle from the far end. Basket over her arm. She barely slows down. Her eyes go straight to the ACO Pine Gel tub on the shelf. She picks it up. One smooth motion. It goes in the basket. |
| **The contrast** | 0:15 | Wide two-shot: Gloria still deliberating between products, trolley overflowing. Ana already walking away, one item in her basket. The visual does all the work. No words needed yet. |

**Voice Over / Sound:**
> *No VO here — let the image breathe. Ambient supermarket sound. The contrast is the message.*

**Director's Note:** This scene lives or dies on the two-shot at 0:15. We need both women visible in the same frame, trolley vs basket. Shoot this wide. Don't rush to dialogue.

---

### SCENE 02 · The Checkout `0:20 – 0:35`

**Setting:** Supermarket checkout. Gloria's belt is stacked. Ana places one tub on the belt beside her.

| Beat | Timecode | Action |
|------|----------|--------|
| **Gloria's belt** | 0:20 | Gloria unloads: bottle after bottle, product after product. The checkout clerk starts scanning — it takes a moment. |
| **Ana places one** | 0:25 | Ana steps up beside her. One green tub on the belt. That's it. She waits, relaxed. |
| **The look** | 0:28 | Gloria glances at Ana's single item, then at her own pile. A beat. She can't help herself. |
| **The exchange** | 0:30 | Gloria: *"That's all you're buying?"* Ana looks at the tub, then back at Gloria. Warm, no judgment. *"That's all I need."* A small smile. She means it. |

**Dialogue:**
> **Gloria:** "That's all you're buying?"  
> **Ana:** "That's all I need."

**Director's Note:** This is the heart of the film. The line must land naturally — not like a punchline, more like a quiet fact. Ana's delivery is kind, never superior. Two takes: one where Gloria's expression shifts to curious, one where she almost smiles. Use the curious one.

---

### SCENE 03 · The Proof `0:35 – 0:50`

**Setting:** Ana's home — kitchen, bathroom, floors. Quick cuts. The ACO tub visible in every shot.

| Beat | Timecode | Action |
|------|----------|--------|
| **Kitchen surface** | 0:35 | ACO tub on counter. Ana wipes the hob. One motion. Done. |
| **Bathroom tiles** | 0:40 | Same tub. She sprays and wipes tiles — they gleam. |
| **Glass surface** | 0:43 | Window or mirror. One application. Streak-free, clear. |
| **Floor** | 0:46 | Ana mops. Wide shot. Tub in the corner of frame. She's not working hard — she's barely trying. The floor is spotless. |
| **Hold on tub** | 0:49 | Close on the ACO tub sitting on a clean counter. Label clear. Alone. Enough. |

**Voice Over:**
> "Kitchen. Bathroom. Glass. Floors. One product does it all."

**Director's Note:** Every cut is 3–4 seconds. The tub must be in frame at all times — it's the through-line. No wipes or transitions between surfaces. Hard cuts only. The speed says efficiency; the tub says simplicity.

### SCENE 04 · End Slate `0:50 – 1:00`

**Setting:** Clean white. Hero packshot. The campaign closes.

| Beat | Timecode | Action |
|------|----------|--------|
| **Cut to white** | 0:50 | The ACO Pine Gel tub rises in from the bottom of frame. Centred. Unhurried. Label fully legible. |
| **Tagline** | 0:54 | "YOU ONLY NEED ONE." builds in, bold and final. |
| **CTA** | 0:57 | "Smarter Cleaning Starts Here." appears below in clean secondary type. |
| **Logo** | 0:58 | ACO red logo settles bottom right. "All Purpose Pine Gel Cleaner · Angola" locks in. Holds to end. |

**Super / Text on Screen:**
> YOU ONLY NEED ONE.  
> Smarter Cleaning Starts Here.  
> ACO Pine Gel · All Purpose Pine Gel Cleaner

**Director's Note:** No music crescendo. Clean, quiet, confident. The white frame after the busy supermarket energy makes the product feel like a relief. Hold longer than feels comfortable — 10 seconds is the goal.
