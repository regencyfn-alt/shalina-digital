THE STORY
What This Film Is About
The Liberation
of the Cupboard

Every Angolan home has that cupboard — the one that bursts open with bleach, mops, sprays, and six different floor cleaners bought for six different jobs. This film is about one woman who discovered she doesn't need any of it. One green tub. That's the whole secret.

Emotional Journey
Chaos → Clarity
Overwhelm → Pride

We open in Gloria's world — chaotic, relatable, a little funny. We pivot to Ana's world — clean, confident, effortless. The contrast does the selling. The audience sees themselves in Gloria and aspires to be Ana. ACO is what makes you Ana.

The Central Insight
One Product.
Every Surface.

ACO Pine Gel is genuinely multi-purpose — it works on kitchen surfaces, floors, and bathrooms. The film doesn't just claim this. It shows it, product on counter, three locations, same tub. The proof is visual, effortless, and true.

SCENE BREAKDOWN
SCENE 01
The Problem
0:00 – 0:10
9:16 · 10 SEC
0:00
Opening frame: Gloria's kitchen. Warm afternoon light. She's cooking. In the background, her cleaning cupboard door is slightly ajar — you can already see products threatening to spill.
0:03
She nudges the cupboard door. Cascade. Mop bucket, floor spray, bleach, stain remover, glass cleaner, scourer — they all come tumbling out at once. She catches them instinctively, arms full, face incredulous.
0:07
Beat to camera. Arms still overflowing with products, she looks directly at us. The look says everything: "This is my life." Slight shake of the head. It's funny. It's real.
Voice Over · Gloria's Inner Thought
"Wait — how many cleaners does one home actually need?"
Director's Note
Play this warm, not slapstick. Gloria is not a fool — she's every woman. The humour comes from recognition, not mockery. Hand-held feel. Medium shot tightening to close on her face for the camera beat.
SCENE 02
The Reveal
0:10 – 0:20
9:16 · 10 SEC
0:10
Hard cut to silence. A cupboard opens. The camera is inside looking out. One green tub sits centred on the shelf, perfectly placed. Nothing else. The contrast with Scene 01 is immediate and sharp.
0:13
Pull back to reveal Ana, standing in her bright kitchen, holding the tub at chest height. Her expression is quiet confidence — not boastful, just knowing. She's discovered something she can't unknow.
0:16
One finger raised. She lifts her index finger toward camera — "this one". Her smile breaks open. Wide, warm, radiant. She has the secret and she's sharing it with us.
Voice Over · Narrator
"Meet ACO Pine Gel. The only cleaner your home needs."
Director's Note
The inside-cupboard shot is the hero frame — linger on it a beat before the reveal. Ana should feel aspirational but accessible. Her energy is warm wisdom, not superiority. The product label must read clearly when she holds it.
SCENE 03
The Proof
0:20 – 0:30
9:16 · 10 SEC
0:20
Kitchen hob. The ACO tub sits on the counter. Ana pours a small amount onto a cloth and wipes the hob with one smooth stroke. The surface gleams. The motion is effortless — this is not work, it's almost elegant.
0:23
Bathroom sink. Same green tub, now beside the basin. Ana scrubs the sink with a fresh cloth — the grime lifts immediately. She steps back and it sparkles. She gives the smallest satisfied nod.
0:27
Floor — wide shot. Ana mops with confidence. The tub is visible in the corner of the frame on a low surface. She doesn't even slow down. Three rooms, one product. The case is closed.
Voice Over
"Kitchen. Bathroom. Floors. One tub — every surface in your home."
Director's Note
The ACO tub must be visible in every cut — it never leaves the frame. Each location cut is exactly 3 seconds. Fast but breathable. The pace builds slightly toward the floor shot. No voiceover fuss — the product does the talking.

Director's Note
No music swell. No sting. The silence after the film gives the tagline room to land. If audio is used, a single clean piano note or a breath of wind — nothing that says "ad". The white should feel like a full stop.
