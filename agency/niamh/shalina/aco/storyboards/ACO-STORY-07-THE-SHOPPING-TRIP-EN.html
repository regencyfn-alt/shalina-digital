Story Brief
The Idea
The Point of Purchase Argument

Two women. Same supermarket aisle. Same cleaning problem to solve. Gloria buys what most people buy — a different product for every surface. Ana buys one green tub and keeps moving. The film doesn't explain the difference. It shows it.

Why This Works
The Exact Moment of Truth

The supermarket aisle is where the buying decision happens — where competing products sit side by side. A confident woman reaching past five options to pick one says more than any claim on a label. The film makes that clarity aspirational.

The Payoff
Six Words. Everything Said.

"That's all you're buying?"
"That's all I need."

Lands because the film earns it visually first. You don't feel like you're being sold to. You feel like you're being let in on something.
