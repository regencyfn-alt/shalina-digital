# ACO Angola — Story 02: O Teste da Gordura
### *The Grease Test*

**Brand:** ACO Multipurpose Household Gel  
**Market:** Angola · Portuguese  
**Format:** 22s · Social/TikTok/IG  
**Characters:** Glória · Ana  
**Status:** Brief  
**Date:** 2026-07-02

---

## THE STORY
*What it is. What happens. What the audience sees. ~200 words.*

Two women. Same kitchen. Same problem: a stovetop caked in old cooking grease — the stubborn kind that doesn't lift on the first pass.

Glória approaches the way most of us do. She has products. Degreaser spray. Dish soap. A multi-surface foam. She tries one, scrubs hard. Switches to the next. The grease moves a little. Not enough. She looks at the stovetop. Back at the bottles. The effort is real and it shows.

Ana sets one tub of ACO on the counter. She pours gel into a small bowl, then adds equal water. She holds the bowl up toward camera and says it simply: *"Um para um — gordura pesada."* One for one. That's the ratio for heavy grease. She dips a cloth. One pass across the stovetop. It clears.

No drama in it. No performance. She rinses the cloth and wipes again to show the surface — clean, dry, done. While Glória is still switching products, Ana's stovetop is already gleaming. Ana doesn't look over. She doesn't need to. She looks at us, raises one finger, and that's the story.

---

## WHY IT WORKS · AUDIENCE
*The emotional and behavioural truth. Why they watch. ~100 words.*

Every household in Angola has a version of Glória's counter — the accumulation of products that promised more than they delivered. The audience recognises it immediately and feels the low-grade frustration of cleaning that takes too long. What stops the scroll is the ratio: *Um para um*. A piece of practical knowledge delivered mid-film, embedded in a moment that already has their attention. They watch Ana not because she wins but because she knows something. By the time the stovetop is clean, they've already registered: there's a right way to use ACO for grease, and now they know it.

---

## WHY IT WORKS · BRAND
*How this serves ACO. ~50 words.*

"Só Precisas de Um" is the campaign truth. This story proves it under pressure — heavy grease is the hardest case, and ACO handles it. The 1:1 dilution ratio makes this educational without making it a tutorial. It extends the campaign into a new usage occasion: the stovetop, the toughest surface in the kitchen.
